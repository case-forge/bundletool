Split for email

      
    

    


    

      
Split by

      

        
        
        

      


      
      

        
        
      

      
18 MB is the usual ceiling: Gmail and Outlook cap the whole message at 25,000,000 bytes, including headers and the roughly 37% that attachments grow in transit, so about 18 MB of PDF is what actually fits.

      
A section that comes to more than this is cut further, at document boundaries, and its parts are labelled "Part 1 of 2". A section under it stays whole, however small.


      

        
How to split

        

          
          
        

      


      
Part labelling

      

        
        
        
      


      
Documents are never split across parts unless one document alone is over the limit. With a cover page or seamless, page numbering continues across parts and index links only work inside part 1; with an index of their own, every part is numbered and linked by itself.

      
Page numbers stay exactly as they are in the whole bundle, including numbering by section. A link to a page in another part cannot work between separate files, so it is left as plain text. With an index of their own, the pages of each part are numbered from 1 instead.
